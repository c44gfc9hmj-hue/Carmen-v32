Deep Dive is three investigation lenses — Bondage, People, Clothing — that
expand THROUGH discovered evidence instead of rewriting “subject + keyword”.

separate from text hits (first-name matches are not identity; competing names
are excluded from the primary visual corpus).

No secrets. No hardcoded Drea answers. No person registry. No UI redesign.
Bondage remains a primary Deep Dive button.
